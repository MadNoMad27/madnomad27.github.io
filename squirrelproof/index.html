SquirrelProof for Mac

Support

SquirrelProof locks you into one app or browser tab until the timer ends. Questions, bugs and ideas: madnomad.apps@proton.me. In the app, Settings ▸ About ▸ Help & feedback starts the email for you with your app and macOS version filled in.

Getting started

  • Start a lock: switch to the app or tab you want to work in, press ⌃⌥L, then Return. Locks run from 5 minutes to 3 hours.
  • A thought pops up mid-lock: press ⌃⌥P to park it, then get back to work. Your parked thoughts wait in the menu and on the end-of-lock screen.
  • Two things at once: in the start panel, press ⌘R and click a second app or tab to read alongside (the Reference). For work across many websites, press ⌘E for a research lock: any site and any number of tabs, except the ones on your blocked list.
  • Try it free: the 2-minute practice lock is always free. You also get 5 free full sessions before the one-time unlock.

Common questions

I can't see SquirrelProof in the menu bar.

A notch or a menu-bar manager can hide it. Press ⌃⌥M to open its menu from anywhere, or open SquirrelProof from Applications or Spotlight. Also check System Settings ▸ Menu Bar: SquirrelProof needs to be allowed there. You can pick a squirrel, husky or acorn icon in Settings ▸ General ▸ Menu bar.

How do I end a lock early?

Click ⋯ on the floating timer and choose End early…. You wait 30 seconds the first time in a day, 2 minutes the second time and 5 minutes after that. The wait gives you a moment to change your mind; going back to your work cancels it.

Why can't I quit SquirrelProof during a lock?

That's on purpose: quitting would be an easy way out. If SquirrelProof is force-quit it comes back within seconds with the same time left, and after a restart the lock resumes. If SquirrelProof itself crashes three times in one lock, the lock ends so you're never stuck.

Safari says SquirrelProof's website access is limited.

In Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Extensions, turn on SquirrelProof, tick Allow in Private Browsing, and set website access to Allow on Every Website. You can also click the SquirrelProof button in Safari's toolbar and choose Allow on every website.

How do I connect Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge?

Open SquirrelProof Settings ▸ Browsers and follow the steps for your browser. It installs the SquirrelProof extension and pairs it with a 6-digit code. Turn on Allow in Incognito (private or InPrivate in some browsers) on the extension's details page.

The extension says "Not running".

Open SquirrelProof. The extension reconnects by itself within a few seconds. If it still doesn't, open Settings ▸ Browsers in SquirrelProof and pair it again.

I bought SquirrelProof on another Mac.

Open Settings ▸ Purchase and click Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I remove SquirrelProof?

Outside a lock, choose Quit SquirrelProof from its menu, then drag the app to the Trash. To remove your data too, delete the folder ~/Library/Group Containers/6HM8CPZ27F.app.blinkers.mac.

Privacy
